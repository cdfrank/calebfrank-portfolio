## The short version

Street Signal turns real places into playable skate spots. You can skate downtown Ann Arbor and Detroit's Hart Plaza today, in a browser on a phone or a desktop.

I carried the idea for a long time. When I started building, I directed AI the way a creative director runs a studio, and kept the calls for myself.
