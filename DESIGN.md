# Design

Near-black ground, off-white ink, one acid lime. Dark and lime come from Caleb's existing identity.

- **Type:** Permanent Marker for the wordmark and short labels only. Bricolage Grotesque for headings, Hanken Grotesk for reading, JetBrains Mono for dates.
- **Signature:** the Record, a chart with one block per project, built from the content files. Lime blocks are case studies, outlined blocks are lab work.
- **Color:** lime marks action and proof. One full lime band on the homepage gives the page its break.
- **Layout:** lists and rules rather than cards. Real work is shown large.
